FAQ

Is it really free to try?

Yes — every install gets a 14-day trial of the full app with no feature restrictions. After it ends you activate with a license key.

Do you collect my data?

No. Snippets, vault secrets and clipboard history never leave your machine. There is no account and no telemetry.

What can I search?

Apps, text snippets, vault credentials and clipboard history — all from the same box. Results appear while you type.

How does activation work?

License keys are cryptographically signed and verified entirely offline on your device — no server lookup, works without an internet connection.

What hotkeys does it use?

Alt+Space by default to open the search bar (configurable), Ctrl+Shift+H for clipboard history, Ctrl+N to create a snippet.
